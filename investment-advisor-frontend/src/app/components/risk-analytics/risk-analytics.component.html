📚 Understanding Risk Metrics

Volatility

Measures how much an asset's price fluctuates. Higher volatility = higher risk but potentially higher returns.

Sharpe Ratio

Measures risk-adjusted return. A Sharpe ratio > 1.0 is considered good. Shows how well you're compensated for taking risk.

Maximum Drawdown

The biggest decline from peak to trough. Useful for understanding psychological pain tolerance and worst-case scenarios.

Value at Risk

Maximum expected loss at a given confidence level. Helps with position sizing and risk management.

Concentration Risk

Risk from having too much in one asset. Diversification across uncorrelated assets reduces this risk.

Stress Testing

Simulates portfolio performance during market crashes. Helps you understand your portfolio's resilience.
